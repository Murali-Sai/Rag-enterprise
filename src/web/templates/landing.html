Part I — Overview
Item 1 Business +
Downloads, parses, and indexes real 10-K filings straight from EDGAR — not synthetic demo text. Ask about revenue, margins, or risk factors and get an answer grounded in the actual filing, with the exact section it came from.
Item 1A Risk Factors +
Every response passes through guardrails before it reaches you: MNPI detection, investment-advice blocking, forward-looking-statement flags, PII redaction. Violations are logged, not just hidden.
Item 2 Properties +
FastAPI, ChromaDB, LangChain, local embeddings. Deployed as a single Cloud Run container with the filing index baked in at build time — no ingestion step on cold start.
Part II — Results & Disclosures
Item 7 Management's Discussion & Analysis +
Measured across 54 hand-written questions in seven strata, judged by GPT-4o-mini. Sixteen of the 54 have no answer in the corpus and are scored on whether the system correctly declines — pooling them into the RAGAS means would lower every figure because the system behaved correctly.

Item 7A Disclosures About Access Risk +
Who can see what is enforced as a ChromaDB filter, not a UI toggle — a research role never receives a trading-desk document, even in the raw retrieval results. Real filings call this section "Market Risk." We're disclosing information-access risk instead — same idea, different exposure.

AAPL · JPM · TSLA · MSFT · GS — each company's latest 10-K, parsed into Item 1 (Business), 1A (Risk Factors), 7 (MD&A), 7A (Market Risk), and 8 (Financials).
